The idea

Konstanz, one letter steadier.

Konstant takes the city's name and changes one letter. The new word means steady, and that is the promise: the app is there whenever you need to move, with signal or without.

Who it's for

People who live, study and visit here. Students between Uni and Altstadt, commuters from Wollmatingen, visitors who just stepped off the train with roaming switched off.

What makes it different

Other apps need the internet to plan a trip. Konstant carries the timetable, the map and the router on the phone. Routes come back in under a second, anywhere in the city.

Brand pillars

Four things Konstant always is.

Steady

Works without internet. Opens instantly. Never a spinner where an answer should be.

Door to door

The bus and the walk on both ends, planned together, with turn-by-turn steps for the last bit.

Honest

Times come straight from the published timetable and are labelled as scheduled. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Local

Built for Konstanz only: its districts on the map, Fasnacht and Seenachtfest on the start screen.

Logo

A K drawn as a journey.

The mark is a capital K built from the app's own map symbols. Read it from the bottom: you start at a stop, join the line and arrive at your destination.

A

The line

A steady vertical stem, 12 units wide. It stands for the network that is always there.

B

The destination

The diamond the app uses for "where you're going". It sits on the curve's tangent so the route flows into it.

C

The start

The open stop ring, the app's symbol for "where you are".

D

The transfer

Where the two arms meet. One trip, joined up.

Built on a 100-unit grid with a 10-unit safe zone. Strokes 12 / 10 / 6.5, round caps. The ring is filled with the background colour so it stays open on any surface.
